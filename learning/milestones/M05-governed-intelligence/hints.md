# M05 Hints

## Hint 1 — Diagnostic question

Which missing piece of evidence should prevent a synthetic decision from being accepted?

## Hint 2 — Concept

A school permission slip is usable only when it names the activity, the approving rule, and who signed it. A missing signature means the student follows the safe alternative.

## Hint 3 — Location

Look for the learner’s synthetic turn-decision interface and the typed Product Knowledge, Sales Policy, provenance, and Handoff boundaries.

## Hint 4 — Structure

Validate the typed supporting records before accepting a synthetic decision. Send every missing or invalid-evidence branch to the same safe Handoff terminal.

## Hint 5 — Pseudocode

Use this incomplete decision sketch:

```text
if [typed knowledge] and [typed policy] and [provenance ____]:
  accept [synthetic decision]
else:
  route to [____]
```
